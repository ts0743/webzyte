About Us

About Webzyte

Webzyte started in 2019 in Hyderabad with a simple idea: most small and mid-sized businesses need websites that work harder than they cost. Not the cheapest option, not the flashiest — just a site that loads fast, ranks reasonably, and turns visitors into customers.

Since then we've worked with "retail brands, healthcare providers, and B2B service companies" across India, handling everything from a first website to full digital marketing programs.
