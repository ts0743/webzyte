About Us

About Webzyte

Webzyte started in 2019 in Hyderabad with a simple idea: most small and mid-sized businesses need websites that work harder than they cost. Not the cheapest option, not the flashiest — just a site that loads fast, ranks reasonably, and turns visitors into customers.

Since then we've worked with "retail brands, healthcare providers, and B2B service companies" across India, handling everything from a first website to full digital marketing programs.

How We Work

We don't hand you a template and call it custom. Every project starts with an actual conversation about what you're trying to achieve — more leads, more sales, a rebrand — and the site or campaign gets built around that goal, not around what looks trendy this year.

Our Team

Webzyte is a team of 15+ designers, developers, and marketers — not a single freelancer wearing five hats, and not a revolving door of subcontractors either. The team is led by founder Krishna Teja.

Where We're Based

Webzyte is headquartered at Vasant Nagar, Hyderabad, and works with clients both locally and remotely.
